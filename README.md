# Sight Reading Trainer

This is a little web app I made to help practice sight-reading music notation. 

I tried musictheory.net's note identification exercises, but found myself wanting to play the notes on a keyboard rather than click a box in the site. This app lets you connect a MIDI keyboard or digital piano to your computer via USB, and then go through several types of sight-reading/note identification exercises where you input your answer by playing the note on an actual keyboard.

In addition to individual notes in the bass and treble clefs (optionally with ledger lines and accidentals), this also includes grand staff exercises, as well as sequences of notes and different key signatures.
